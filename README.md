# Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406) bounty entry. One tombstone epitaph line, at most 150 characters, written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.ts`.

## The entry

> My last words were much funnier than this. Blame the stonecutter: he charged by the letter.

The joke turns on the medium itself: the dead man complains his real last words were funnier, and blames the stonecutter — who charged by the letter, which is exactly why the line is so short. Dying speech, carved in stone, twist landing on the last word.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
